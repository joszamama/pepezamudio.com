This thursday took place at ETSII Seville the defense of the Master’s thesis of one of the [Diverso Lab](https://grupo.us.es/diversolab) students, José Antonio Zamudio. I have successfully defended my master’s thesis entitled “Chatbot Generation Using Software Product Line Techniques: A Systematic Approach for Reusing Domain Artifacts”, which is the first systematic approach for the intelligent reuse of domain artifacts in the context of chatbot generation that exists in the literature.

This new line of research proposes synergies between the field of product lines, modeling and artificial intelligence and shows a first approach by using a tool for the construction of intelligent chatbots by reusing information and applying techniques from product line engineering. In this way the student concludes his master studies and prepares to start his doctoral thesis.
