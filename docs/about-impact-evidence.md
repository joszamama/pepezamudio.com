# Evidence behind the About timeline

The About page shows a few results that can be understood on a quick scan. This review covers every paper copy currently hosted on the site. Claims about performance stay within the evaluated subjects; preprints are labeled as such. The papers not selected for a timeline headline remain in the Research bibliography.

| Paper | Main conclusion or result | About timeline decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Shaping Test Inputs in Grammar-Based Fuzzing](../public/papers/shaping-test-inputs-grammar-based-fuzzing.pdf) · ISSTA 2024 doctoral symposium | The JSON proof of concept generated 1,000 inputs with uniform age and normally distributed budget values while respecting value constraints. Bug-finding impact was left for future evaluation. | Selected: control over input distributions. The prototype count is supporting evidence, not the contribution itself. |
| [Fandango: Evolving Language-Based Testing](../public/papers/fandango-evolving-language-based-testing.pdf) · ISSTA 2025 | Across five ISLa benchmarks, Fandango produced inputs 29–1,355 times faster, with 99.27–100% parser acceptance. | Selected: practical, faster generation of constrained valid inputs. These are benchmark results, not a universal speedup. |
| [XAVIER](../public/papers/xavier-xml-injection-early-author-draft.pdf) · ISSTA 2025 | The early author draft reports WSDL operation coverage comparable to SoapUI Pro on eight public service definitions and an open-source approach to XML injection test generation. The local draft predates the published version. | Selected: turn WSDL contracts into XML injection tests. The comparison concerns specified functionality coverage, not discovered vulnerabilities. |
| [Personalized Fuzzing on a GNSS Module](../public/papers/personalized-fuzzing-gnss.pdf) · FUZZING 2025 workshop short paper | Fandango generated 100 tailored UBX inputs in 5.2–10.3 seconds for a hardware case study, including malformed edge cases. The module acknowledged valid messages and rejected invalid ones in that run. | Selected: domain-specific edge-case testing on real GNSS hardware. The paper does not establish scalability or report a discovered device vulnerability. |
| [Constraint-Driven Fuzzing at Scale](../public/papers/constraint-driven-fuzzing-at-scale.pdf) · ICST 2026 | Presents Fandango as an installable tool and describes classroom and industry use. Its performance table summarizes the earlier ISSTA evaluation. | Not repeated as a separate speed headline. |
| [Search-Based Generation of Complex Inputs with Fandango](../public/papers/ssbse-2026-search-based-generation.pdf) · SSBSE 2026 | Explains evolutionary search over grammar derivation trees and summarizes the 29–1,355 times benchmark result. | Research framing in the PhD summary; performance linked to the original ISSTA study. |
| [FSE 2026 Fandango tutorial](../public/papers/fse-2026-fandango-tutorial.pdf) | The hands-on tutorial covers grammars, constraints, and protocol testing. Its content had been delivered to more than 100 RIO Summer School participants. | Selected: hands-on teaching of the methods. The count refers to the summer school, not the FSE session. |
| [Feature-Focused Test Generation](../public/papers/feature-focused-test-generation.pdf) · ASE 2026 | In ten evaluated formats, one or two feature slices matched the full slice suite’s aggregate observed line coverage. For PNG, one of 29 slices sufficed. Focused inputs reached their intended code region at a 100% mean rate versus 26% for unfocused inputs. | Selected: focused tests that retain measured coverage and reliably reach targeted code. Coverage equality does not establish equal fault-detection power. |
| [Language-Based Protocol Testing](../public/papers/language-based-protocol-testing.pdf) · public preprint | Interaction grammars model messages and state for SMTP, DNS, and FTP. In one lightftp case study, Fandango reached 80.3% statement coverage in about 13.5 minutes; AFLNET reached a median 69.6% after 24 hours. The tools covered partly different code. | Selected: generate stateful protocol conversations. Keep the FTP result scoped to one case study and label the preprint. |
| [High-Performance Generation of Constrained Inputs](../public/papers/high-performance-constrained-inputs.pdf) · arXiv v3, revised July 2026 | The paper's primary result combines grammar-to-Rust compilation with multi-objective evolutionary search to solve complex constraints. In its constrained C-subset case study, Fandango-RS generated a mean 877 valid programs in about a minute; the earlier Fandango and single-objective Fandango-RS settings produced none. Core operations were 3–4 orders of magnitude faster than Fandango's. The separate compiler discrepancies are a secondary case study. | Selected: complex compiler-test constraints made feasible. State that 3–4 orders refers to core operations, not end-to-end fuzzing. The arXiv HTML abstract still says 401, but the hosted v3 PDF reports 877. |
| [Finding the Minimum k-Weighted Dominating Sets](../public/papers/minimum-k-weighted-dominating-sets.pdf) · journal article | Iterated Greedy found optimal or near-optimal solutions quickly; for the unweighted special case, comparable results took about half the computation time of the compared method. The published paper lists CISPA as José A. Zamudio's affiliation. | Selected for the PhD timeline as a continuation of the Universidad Pablo de Olavide collaboration, with its weighted-graph contribution first. The half-time result applies to the unweighted special case. |
| [Analysis Operations on the Run](../public/papers/feature-model-analysis-constraint-based-recommenders.pdf) · SPLC 2023 | Proposes and demonstrates feature-model metrics for recommendation accessibility and restrictiveness. | Included in the complete Research bibliography; outside the PhD and visit timeline entries. |
